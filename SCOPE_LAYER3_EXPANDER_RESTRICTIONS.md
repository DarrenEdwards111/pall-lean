# Scope: Expander / pseudorandom restrictions for branching holography — the independence audit

**Question (HAL's first concrete task):** can the Layer-2 AC⁰ restriction/survivor proof be rerun with
**k-wise / pseudorandom / expander-generated** restrictions instead of fully independent `p`-random
restrictions? **Where exactly is independence used** — star tails, `pweight` factorization, union
bounds, decoder injectivity? *If independence is only in tails/weights, expanders may help; if it is
deep in the reconstruction measure, harder.*

**Framing (honest):** this is a candidate *Layer-3 ingredient* — a derandomisation/amplification layer,
**not** a replacement for the polynomial-method machinery that AC⁰[p] actually needs (MOD/threshold
gates need algebra, not restrictions). It also is **not** P vs NP. Scope only; no brick built here.

## The measure and exactly where independence enters

`pweight p ρ = ∏ v : Fin n, (if ρ v = none then p else (1-p)/2)` — a **product measure** (full
coordinate independence). Auditing every consumer:

**Verdict on the structure:** independence lives in exactly two places — **(1) the global star tails**
(`stars_pgf` and its Markov/Chernoff corollaries) and **(2) the per-label weight ratio**
(`pweight_le_ratio_pow`). The *reconstruction / decoder* — the actual holographic core (injectivity,
label count, geometric assembly, unions) — is **independence-free**. This is the favourable case HAL
hypothesised.

## Why the favourable case is genuinely favourable (the bounded-support observation)

The two independence sites have very different difficulty under a pseudorandom measure:

1. **Weight ratio — `s`-wise independence suffices *exactly*.** `pweight_le_ratio_pow` only compares two
   restrictions `σ ⊒ τ` differing on the **freed set of one descent**, whose size is bounded by the
   depth threshold `s` (small — `s = polylog` in the regime). A measure that is **`s`-wise independent**
   reproduces the *exact* marginal on any `≤ s` coordinates, so the ratio
   `∏_{freed}(p/((1-p)/2)) = (2p/(1-p))^{#freed}` holds **verbatim**. No approximation needed: branching
   holography only ever weighs **bounded-support events** (paths of length `≤ s`), and bounded-support
   events see only `≤ s`-wise marginals. *This is the crux that makes pseudorandom restrictions plausible.*

2. **Star tails — need a concentration substitute (the real work).** `stars_pgf` is a genuine `n`-coordinate
   product; `P[stars < s]`, `P[stars ≥ F]` are global tail events. Under a pseudorandom measure these are
   re-derived by either:
   - **`k`-wise concentration:** `k`-wise-independent Chernoff/moment bounds on `stars = ∑_v 1[free_v]`
     (standard; `k = O(log)` gives polynomially-small tails), or
   - **expander mixing:** if the free/fixed pattern is generated by a Ramanujan walk, the spectral gap
     bounds the deviation of `stars` from its mean (expander-walk Chernoff, Gillman).

## Answer to the question

- **Independence is used only in tails/weights — NOT in the reconstruction.** So per HAL's criterion,
  **expanders / k-wise restrictions are a viable ingredient**, not blocked by the decoder.
- The **decoder/injectivity/count is reusable verbatim** (measure-free).
- The **weight ratio is reusable verbatim under `s`-wise independence** (bounded support).
- The **star tails are the only piece needing genuine re-derivation**, via `k`-wise concentration or
  expander-walk mixing — a contained, standard analytic task.

So the honest formulation HAL proposed — *"expander-holographic restrictions: restriction process from a
Ramanujan walk; prove switching/survivor bounds still hold under spectral mixing; then combine with the
polynomial method for MOD gates"* — is **structurally supported**: the holographic core survives, only
the tails change measure.

## Caveats (do not oversell)

- This makes the *AC⁰* restriction step derandomisable; it does **nothing** to MOD/threshold gates. For
  AC⁰[p] the core engine remains **Razborov–Smolensky** (`SCOPE_LAYER3_AC0p.md`); expander restrictions
  would be an auxiliary derandomisation/amplification layer, used *before* the polynomial method on the
  surviving AC⁰ structure.
- "`s`-wise independence suffices" is established here at the level of *where the product is used*; a full
  proof must also check the **decoder's choice of deep input `x`** (the `exists_deep_input` / value
  recording in [155b]) does not secretly re-introduce a global-independence requirement. First audit
  target if this is pursued: confirm `descentPosValLabels`'s `x`-dependence is itself bounded-support.

## CORRECTION (after attempting to supply the `s`-wise instance) — the favourable case was overstated

Building the measure abstraction (`RestrictionMeasure`, `descent_switching_prob_measure`) and then
trying to instantiate it `s`-wise forced two findings that **correct §"the favourable observation"
above**. Recording them honestly rather than fabricating an instance:

1. **The weight ratio is NOT bounded-support — it runs over the full descent path (length `s..n`).**
   In the count (`descent_switching_prob_measure`, step 1) the ratio `μ.ratio` is applied at
   `s_param = pathLen`, the *full* freed count of one descent; the count itself bounds path lengths in
   `Finset.Icc s n` (verified: `L.flatten.length ∈ Icc s n`). So the relevant events are paths of
   length **`≥ s`, up to `n`** — the opposite of the "`≤ s`" claimed above. The ratio therefore needs
   the product structure over **up to `n`** coordinates; a small-`k` `k`-wise-independent measure does
   **not** reproduce it. *(My earlier "branching holography only weighs bounded-support events" was
   backwards.)*

2. **The exact interface forces `μ = pweight`.** (M2) `∑_{extBox τ} μ = ((1-p)/2)^{n-stars τ}` for
   *all* `τ` determines every conditional mass; Möbius inversion over the extension poset then pins
   `μ(σ)` uniquely — to the product measure. So the exact `RestrictionMeasure` interface is inhabited
   **only** by `pweight`. There is no genuine non-product instance to supply against it.

### What survives, and what does not

- **Survives:** the decoder/injectivity/label-count is measure-free (verified, unchanged); and the
  abstraction `descent_switching_prob_measure` is a *faithful generalisation* that recovers the
  original (`descent_switching_prob_findep_via_measure`) — a clean, correct refactor.
- **Does not survive:** the claim that an `s`-wise / expander measure *drops in*. The weight ratio's
  full-path support means independence is used over `Θ`(path length) ≤ `n` coordinates, not a constant
  `≤ s` window.

### The honest open direction (not a drop-in)

The long paths are individually `r^{pathLen}`-suppressed and rare. A genuine derandomisation would
keep `k`-wise independence and **separately bound the total weight of length-`> k` descents** by a
`k`-wise moment/tail argument (the short descents, length `≤ k`, keep the exact product ratio). That is
real new analysis — *not* a `RestrictionMeasure` instance, and *not* implied by the current interface.
Status: **open; the favourable drop-in does not exist.**

*Conclusion: the abstraction brick is sound and useful as a refactor; the `s`-wise substitution it was
scoped to enable does not go through as first hoped. No instance fabricated.*
